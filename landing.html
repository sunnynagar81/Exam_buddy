Features

Everything in one place

From uploading notes to taking quizzes, everything is based on your subject's notes.

📤

Upload your notes

Save PDF notes by subject. Each file's data is stored separately.

💬

Ask questions

Get simple explanations, ask follow-up questions, and see the source page numbers.

📝

Smart summaries

Get short summaries and important points for each file or chapter.

🧠

Practice quizzes

How it works

4 simple steps

Follow these steps the first time you use the app.

1

Enter a subject and upload notes

Type a subject name in the app, upload your PDF, then click "Save and index".

2

Ask questions

Type any question in the Ask tab. Source page numbers appear with each answer.

3

Get a summary

Select a file in the sidebar, then create revision notes in the Summary tab.

4

Take a quiz

Choose the number of questions and difficulty, then check your answers.
